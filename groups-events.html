The Process

How It Works

Four easy steps from "we should do this" to everyone leaving your event feeling incredible.

1

Reach Out

Call, text, or fill out our contact form. Tell us about your event — headcount, vibe, location, and date. The more details, the better we can plan for your crew.

2

Custom Plan

We build your group menu and schedule around your event. A $300 deposit locks your date and secures your spot. Book at least 2 weeks in advance.

3

We Show Up

Your licensed provider arrives at your location with everything needed. Setup takes about 15 minutes — then we're ready to drip. No venue changes required on your end.

4

Everyone Gets Their Drip

45–60 minute sessions, fully customized per person. We can run multiple people simultaneously. Everyone leaves hydrated, energized, and feeling amazing.

The Benefits

Why Group IV Just Makes Sense

IV therapy isn't just for individuals. When your whole crew gets dripped together, the results — and the experience — are on another level.

⚡

Instant Energy & Hydration

IV fluids and vitamins hit your bloodstream directly — no waiting for supplements to kick in. The whole group feels the difference within the session.

🔄

Faster Recovery

Whether it's a hangover, travel fatigue, or post-game soreness — IV therapy cuts recovery time significantly so your crew is back in action fast.

🧠

Mental Clarity & Focus

Our brain-boosting drip options (B-complex, magnesium, NAD+) sharpen focus and lift the fog. Perfect for corporate teams before big meetings or events.

✨

Morale Boost — It's a Vibe

Group IV is an experience, not just a treatment. It's fun, it's social, and it's something your team or guests will be talking about long after the event.

🏥

Reduces Sick Days

Regular IV wellness sessions for corporate teams mean stronger immune systems, fewer sick days, and a workforce that actually shows up feeling well.

🎯

Fully Customizable Per Person

No one-size-fits-all here. Each person in your group chooses (or we recommend) a drip based on their individual needs — everyone gets exactly what they need.
