Why IV Therapy

Not All Hydration Is Created Equal

When you swallow a vitamin, your gut absorbs maybe 30-50% of it on a good day. With IV therapy, the nutrients go directly into your bloodstream -- 100% bioavailable, no waiting for your digestive system to cooperate. The difference is real, and most people feel it within 30-60 minutes of starting a drip.

Brittany Adams, APRN FNP-C oversees every infusion at Happy Drip. You'll get a brief intake before your drip so she knows what you're there for and can customize if needed. Then you sit back, relax, and let it do its thing.

No guesswork, no generic drip-and-go. Just a clinical eye on every patient.
