What We Treat

Acne Doesn't Have One Cause -- Or One Fix

We start by figuring out which type (or combination) you're dealing with, since the right treatment depends on it.

🌙

Hormonal Acne

Breakouts along the jawline, chin, and lower cheeks that flare around your cycle -- often driven by androgens and sometimes linked to PCOS.

🔴

Cystic Acne

Deep, painful, under-the-skin breakouts that don't come to a head and often leave marks or scarring if left untreated.

🧴

Adult-Onset Acne

Acne that shows up (or comes back) well past your teenage years, frequently tied to hormones, stress, or medication changes.

Why Am I Breaking Out?

Most Adult Acne Has a Hormonal Component

If you're past your teens and still breaking out -- especially along the jawline or chin, or in a pattern that tracks with your cycle -- there's usually more going on than skin care alone can fix. Excess androgens, insulin resistance, and thyroid dysfunction are all common, treatable drivers.

We start with a real evaluation, not a guess, so treatment actually targets what's causing your breakouts.

Common Underlying Drivers

  • Hormonal shifts tied to your menstrual cycle
  • PCOS or elevated androgen levels
  • Insulin resistance
  • Thyroid dysfunction
  • Stress-related cortisol changes
  • Certain medications or supplements

  • ✓Your breakouts cluster along the jawline, chin, or track with your cycle

Important Considerations

  • ✗Acne treatment takes 6–8+ weeks to show visible results -- it's not overnight
  • ✗Some prescription options aren't safe during pregnancy or while trying to conceive -- we'll screen for this
  • ✗Severe scarring or cystic acne may benefit from an in-person skin exam and possible dermatology referral
  • ✗Consistency with your treatment plan matters -- results depend on it
