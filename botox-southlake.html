What Southlake Patients Can Expect

Consultation

New aesthetic patients get a brief intake. Brittany reviews your goals, looks at the areas you want treated, and gives you an honest read on what will and won't help. No hard sell.

Treatment Day

The injection itself is quick -- most Jeuveau appointments take 20-30 minutes from intake to out the door. Some minor bruising is possible; makeup is fine the same day.

Jeuveau kicks in over 5-14 days and typically lasts 3-4 months. Brittany schedules a touchup window at your appointment so you never let it fully wear off if you don't want to.
