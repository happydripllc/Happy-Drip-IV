What We Prescribe

Birth Control Options We Manage

We'll review your health history, goals, and any current concerns to find the right option -- whether you're starting for the first time or just need a refill on something that's working.

💊

Combined Oral Contraceptives

The standard combination pill (estrogen + progestin) -- offered in a variety of formulations to match your cycle needs, side-effect history, and lifestyle preferences. Great for those also managing hormonal acne or period irregularity.

🌞

Progestin-Only Pill (Mini-Pill)

A good fit if estrogen isn't right for you -- including those who are breastfeeding, have a history of migraines with aura, or prefer to avoid estrogen for other health reasons.

⚡

Contraceptive Patch

A weekly patch you apply to the skin -- delivers the same hormones as the combination pill without a daily pill to remember. We'll walk you through placement, rotation, and what to do if a patch falls off.

💕

Vaginal Ring

A flexible, monthly ring inserted at home that delivers low-dose estrogen and progestin. We can prescribe the ring for patients who prefer a once-a-month approach over a daily or weekly routine.

📋

Contraceptive Counseling

Not sure which method is right for you? We'll review every option -- pill, patch, ring, shot, IUD, implant, and non-hormonal methods -- and help you make an informed decision based on your health history and priorities.

⌛

Emergency Contraception Guidance

We can discuss emergency contraception options, timing, effectiveness, and answer your questions without judgment. We want you to have accurate information when it matters most.
