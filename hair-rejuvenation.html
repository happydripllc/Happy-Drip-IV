Understanding Hair Loss

There Is Almost Always a "Why"

Most hair loss is not random. It is driven by identifiable -- and often treatable -- root causes: hormonal shifts, nutritional deficiencies, scalp inflammation, circulation issues, or genetics. Treating the surface without addressing the cause leads to temporary results at best.

At Happy Drip IV & Wellness, we take a comprehensive approach: identify contributing factors through labs and history, then layer the therapies most likely to address your specific pattern and cause.

⚖️Hormonal imbalance
🧬Androgenetic pattern
🩸Iron or ferritin deficiency
🫧Thyroid dysfunction
😓Chronic stress / cortisol
💊Medication side effects
🔬Scalp inflammation
🥗Nutritional gaps

Signs We Look For

  • Gradual thinning at the crown or temples
  • Wider part line or visible scalp
  • Excessive shedding (more than ~100 strands/day)
  • Hairline recession at the front
  • Diffuse thinning across the entire scalp
  • Brittle, breakage-prone hair
  • Patchy loss (may indicate alopecia areata)
  • Hair loss alongside fatigue, weight changes, or skin changes
Our Treatment Menu

Seven Pathways to Fuller Hair

We customize every hair rejuvenation plan. Most patients benefit from a combination of two or more approaches. Your provider will recommend the right stack at your consultation.

🪡
In-Office

Scalp Microneedling

Controlled micro-channels in the scalp trigger a healing cascade -- increased blood flow, growth factor release, and stem cell activation in the hair follicle. A proven mechanical stimulus for dormant follicles.

  • Stimulates follicle regeneration directly
  • Enhances absorption of topicals applied during session
  • Often combined with PRP or exosomes for maximum effect
  • Series of 4--6 sessions recommended
🩸
In-Office

PRP Injections

Platelet-rich plasma is drawn from your own blood, concentrated, and injected directly into the scalp. The growth factors in PRP signal follicles to exit the resting phase and return to active growth.

  • Uses your own biology -- no foreign substances
  • Directly activates hair follicle stem cells
  • Can be used alone or with scalp microneedling
  • Results continue building for 3--6 months
⚗️
Special Order

Exosome Therapy

Exosomes deliver billions of growth factors and regenerative signals directly to follicle cells -- without a blood draw. They carry more signaling molecules per vial than PRP and have a potent anti-inflammatory effect that reduces scalp conditions contributing to loss.

  • Billions of growth factors per treatment
  • Especially effective for inflammatory hair loss
  • No blood draw required
  • Can be combined with microneedling for deeper delivery
Exosomes are special-ordered and typically take 2--3 weeks to receive. Plan ahead when booking.
🧬
Peptide Protocol

GHK-Cu & Peptides

GHK-Cu (copper peptide) is one of the most studied peptides for hair growth -- it stimulates follicle enlargement, prolongs the growth phase, and has strong anti-inflammatory properties. We offer both topical and injectable peptide protocols tailored to your needs.

  • Enlarges miniaturized follicles
  • Extends the anagen (growth) phase
  • Reduces scalp inflammation
  • Pairs well with microneedling for enhanced penetration
💡
In-Office

Red Light Therapy

Low-level laser / red light (LLLT) energizes hair follicle cells by stimulating mitochondrial activity. More energy in the follicle cell means stronger, more active growth. FDA-cleared for androgenetic alopecia in both men and women.

  • Stimulates mitochondrial function in follicle cells
  • Improves scalp circulation
  • Painless, non-invasive -- zero downtime
  • Complements all other pathways
🔬
Medical

Hormone, Vitamin & Lab Optimization

Hair loss is frequently driven by something systemic -- low ferritin, thyroid dysfunction, elevated androgens, or vitamin D deficiency. We run a comprehensive lab panel, interpret results in context, and correct deficiencies that are silently blocking your hair growth.

  • Full thyroid panel (TSH, free T3, free T4)
  • Ferritin, iron, and complete blood count
  • Hormonal workup (testosterone, DHEA, estrogen)
  • Vitamin D, B12, zinc, and more
💊
Prescription

Prescription Medications

When appropriate, we can prescribe evidence-based medications that complement your non-prescription treatments. These may include topical or oral options depending on your hair loss pattern, labs, and medical history.

  • Topical and oral options available
  • Tailored to your pattern and lab results
  • Managed alongside other therapies for synergy
  • Ongoing monitoring included in your plan
How It Works

Your Hair Rejuvenation Journey

1

Consultation & Labs

We review your history, pattern, and timeline. Labs are ordered to rule out systemic causes and identify nutritional or hormonal drivers.

2

Personalized Plan

Your provider builds a customized protocol -- selecting the right combination of in-office treatments, peptides, and medical management for your specific case.

3

Treatment Series

In-office sessions are spaced 4--6 weeks apart. At-home protocols run concurrently. We adjust as your hair responds over the first few months.

4

Monitor & Maintain

Most patients see visible improvement at 3--6 months. We reassess labs, adjust the plan, and establish a maintenance cadence to hold and continue your results.

Is This Right for Me?

Good Candidates & Considerations

Hair rejuvenation works for a wide range of patterns and causes. Here's a general guide -- your provider will give a personalized assessment at your consultation.

Great Candidates

  • ✓Gradual thinning or diffuse hair loss
  • ✓Androgenetic alopecia (male or female pattern)
  • ✓Post-partum hair shedding
  • ✓Hair loss tied to hormonal changes or deficiencies
  • ✓Early-to-moderate hair loss (follicles still present)
  • ✓Patients wanting to avoid or delay medications
  • ✓Those already on medications wanting to boost results

When to Discuss First

  • !Completely bald areas with no follicular activity (follicles must be present)
  • !Active scalp infections or open wounds
  • !Certain autoimmune conditions (discuss at consult)
  • !Blood thinning medications prior to PRP (temporary pause may be needed)
  • !Pregnancy (some treatments deferred)
