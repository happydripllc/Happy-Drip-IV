What Is Hyperhidrosis?

It's Not Just "Running Hot." It's a Medical Condition.

Hyperhidrosis is excessive sweating that goes beyond what the body needs for temperature regulation. It can affect the underarms, palms, soles of the feet, forehead, or other areas -- often regardless of heat, exertion, or stress level.

If you're changing shirts mid-day, avoiding handshakes, soaking through fabric without warning, or planning your wardrobe around where you might sweat -- this is more than a minor inconvenience. And it's treatable.

  • ✓ Sweat that soaks through clothing without physical exertion
  • ✓ Visible sweat on the palms during everyday activities
  • ✓ Excessive forehead or scalp sweating in normal temperatures
  • ✓ Antiperspirants that no longer work -- even clinical-strength
  • ✓ Social anxiety or avoidance tied to sweating
The Treatment

How Neurotoxin Stops Excessive Sweating

Neurotoxin injections work by temporarily blocking the chemical signal that tells sweat glands to activate. When that signal is interrupted, the glands in the treated area simply don't produce sweat the same way they did before.

The injections are placed just under the skin in a grid pattern across the treatment area. It's a quick, in-office procedure -- most appointments take under an hour from start to finish. No general anesthesia, no significant downtime.

1

Consultation

We review your history, confirm the treatment area, and answer your questions.

2

Mapping & Prep

The treatment area is cleaned and marked with a grid to guide precise, even placement.

3

Injections

Small amounts of neurotoxin are injected just under the skin in a series of points across the mapped area.

4

Back to Your Day

Most patients resume normal activity immediately. Full results develop over 1-2 weeks.

Preparing for Treatment

Before & After Your Appointment

Before Your Visit

  • For underarm treatments: avoid shaving the area for 24 hours prior
  • Do not apply deodorant, antiperspirant, or skincare products to the treatment area on the day of your visit
  • Come with clean, dry skin
  • Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that gives easy access to the treatment area
  • Let us know about any medications, supplements, or blood thinners you're taking

After Your Treatment

  • Avoid rubbing or massaging the treated area for the remainder of the day
  • Skip intense exercise and excessive heat (saunas, hot tubs) for 24 hours
  • Avoid applying deodorant or other products to the underarms for 24 hours post-treatment
  • Mild redness or tenderness at injection sites is normal and typically resolves quickly
  • Results take 1--2 weeks to fully develop -- don't judge results the same day
