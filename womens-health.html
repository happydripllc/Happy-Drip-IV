Women's Health Services at Happy Drip

Hormone Health ♀

Hormone Balancing & HRT

Lab-guided hormone therapy for perimenopause, menopause, and hormonal imbalance -- designed around your bloodwork, not a one-size-fits-all protocol.

  • Comprehensive hormone panel before any treatment
  • Estrogen, progesterone, and testosterone evaluation
  • Perimenopause and menopause symptom management
  • Mood, sleep, energy, libido, and weight concerns addressed
  • Ongoing monitoring with regular follow-up labs

Metabolic Health 📈

PCOS Management

A thorough approach to polycystic ovary syndrome -- from proper diagnosis and lab evaluation to medication management and lifestyle support.

  • Complete PCOS lab panel (androgens, insulin, thyroid)
  • Irregular cycle and ovulation support
  • Insulin resistance and metabolic management
  • Unwanted hair growth addressed (medical + aesthetic options)
  • Weight management integrated when appropriate

Thyroid Health 🔥

Thyroid Optimization

Feeling exhausted, gaining weight, losing hair, or just not feeling right? Comprehensive thyroid evaluation goes beyond a basic TSH to find the real picture.

  • Full thyroid panel -- TSH, Free T3, Free T4, antibodies
  • Hypothyroid and Hashimoto's management
  • Medication management and dose optimization
  • Symptom-driven approach -- labs alone don't tell the whole story
  • Hair loss, fatigue, and weight concerns taken seriously
