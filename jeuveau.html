The New Tox

What Is Jeuveau®?

Jeuveau® (prabotulinumtoxinA-xvfs) is an FDA-approved botulinum toxin type A — but unlike BOTOX®, it was developed from day one with a singular focus: aesthetic results. Using a modern Hi-Pure™ manufacturing process, Jeuveau® delivers a highly purified formulation specifically designed for cosmetic use.

Like all neurotoxins, Jeuveau® works by temporarily blocking nerve signals to targeted facial muscles, allowing them to relax. The result? Softer lines, smoother skin, and a refreshed appearance — without a "frozen" look. The procedure itself is quick, comfortable, and requires no downtime.

Aftercare & Prep

Pre & Post Care

A little preparation goes a long way toward the best results and smoothest recovery.

Before Your Appointment

  • ✓ Come with a clean, makeup-free face on the day of your appointment
  • ✓ Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours before your treatment
  • ✓ Avoid blood-thinning supplements (fish oil, vitamin E, aspirin, NSAIDs) for 1 week prior, if medically safe for you to do so
  • ✓ Consult your doctor before stopping any prescribed blood-thinning medication — never stop without medical guidance
  • ✓ Let us know about any skin infections, cold sores, or active breakouts near the treatment area

After Your Treatment

  • ✓ No strenuous exercise for 24 hours post-treatment — keep your heart rate down
  • ✓ Avoid excessive heat — saunas, hot tubs, and steam rooms for 24 hours
  • ✓ Do not lie flat or bend forward for at least 4 hours after your appointment
  • ✓ Do not rub, massage, or apply pressure to the treated areas for 24 hours
  • ✓ Results take 2–3 days to appear and reach full effect at 7–14 days — be patient with your glow!
