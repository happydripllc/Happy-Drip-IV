Is This You?

PCOS Doesn't Look the Same for Everyone

PCOS is a hormonal condition, not just a fertility issue — and it's diagnosed by a combination of symptoms and labs, not one single test. That's exactly why it gets missed so often: a "normal" hormone panel doesn't rule it out if the right markers weren't checked, and irregular periods alone are frequently written off as stress.

We look at the full picture — cycle history, symptoms, and a complete hormone panel — before making any diagnosis or building a plan.

Common Signs of PCOS

  • Irregular, infrequent, or absent periods
  • Difficulty losing weight despite consistent effort
  • Acne along the jawline, chin, or chest
  • Excess facial or body hair growth (hirsutism)
  • Thinning hair on the scalp
  • Difficulty getting pregnant
  • Skin tags or darkened skin patches (neck, armpits)
  • Mood changes tied to hormonal fluctuation
