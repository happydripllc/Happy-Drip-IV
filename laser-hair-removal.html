How It Works

The Science Behind Smooth Skin

Laser hair removal works through a process called selective photothermolysis. In plain English: the laser targets the pigment (melanin) inside each hair follicle, heats it up, and damages the follicle enough to significantly slow -- or permanently stop -- regrowth. The surrounding skin stays completely unaffected.

Because hair grows in cycles, multiple sessions are needed to catch follicles in their active growth phase. Most clients see 70--90% permanent reduction after a full treatment series. Maintenance sessions can address any stragglers that pop back up.

Laser hair removal works best on darker hair against lighter skin -- the laser targets melanin contrast. It's less effective on very light, blonde, red, or gray hair since those follicles have little to no pigment for the laser to target.
