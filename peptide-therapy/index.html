What Is Peptide Therapy

Your body already speaks this language.

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as messengers in the body. They tell cells to repair, regenerate, produce hormones, reduce inflammation, or protect tissue. Unlike synthetic drugs, most peptides work WITH your body's existing pathways rather than overriding them.

Brittany prescribes individualized peptide protocols based on your labs, goals, and health history through licensed 503A compounding pharmacies. You get a real prescription from a real provider -- not a research-grade product shipped from a grey-market website.

The basics

  • Short chains of amino acids -- the building blocks your body already uses to communicate between cells
  • Work with your body's existing pathways rather than overriding or suppressing them
  • Prescribed and monitored by a licensed provider -- Brittany Adams, APRN FNP-C -- through a 503A compounding pharmacy
