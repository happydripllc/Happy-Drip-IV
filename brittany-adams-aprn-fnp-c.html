Medical Weight Management

I know this one from both sides.

I’ve personally lost more than 160 pounds, so when I tell you weight management is rarely as simple as “eat less, move more,” I’m not saying it from the other side of a desk.

That doesn’t replace clinical training, and it isn’t a prediction of your results. What it does mean is that I’ll be realistic with you about what medication can and can’t do, and your plan is built around your labs, your history, and your goals.

Individual results vary. Treatment isn’t appropriate for everyone.

How I approach it

  • Medication when it’s appropriate, such as semaglutide or tirzepatide, and not when it isn’t
  • Nutrition and protein intake, not just the number on the scale
  • Attention to preserving lean mass
  • Labs that guide the plan
  • Side-effect management, including our GLP-1 side effects guide
  • Realistic expectations and ongoing follow-up
