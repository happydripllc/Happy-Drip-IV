The Science of Glow

What Is MesoTox?

MesoTox is a Korean-origin aesthetic technique that combines the collagen-stimulating power of microneedling with a hyperdiluted neurotoxin -- delivered directly into the dermis, not the muscle. The result? Your skin gets smarter, not stiffer.

Traditional Botox targets the muscles underneath your skin to smooth wrinkles by limiting movement. MesoTox works differently: by depositing tiny amounts of neurotoxin into the dermal layer at just 0.25mm depth, it regulates sebum production, minimizes pore size, tightens fine skin texture, and creates that signature Korean "glass skin" radiance -- all without touching a single facial muscle.

The microneedling portion creates controlled micro-channels that drive the neurotoxin deep into the dermis while simultaneously triggering collagen remodeling. You get a two-for-one: the glow boost of microneedling and the pore-refining, sebum-controlling effects of neurotoxin -- in a single 30-45 minute session with zero downtime.

This is skin quality maintenance at its finest. No frozen forehead. No "done" look. Just luminous, poreless, dewy skin that looks like yours -- but better.

The Process

Three Steps to Glass Skin

Simple, clinical, effective -- and done in under an hour.

1

Prep & Cleanse

We cleanse your skin, apply a topical numbing cream, and prep the neurotoxin to a precise hyperdiluted concentration. The lower the concentration, the more it behaves as a dermal treatment rather than a muscle blocker.

2

Microneedling Delivery

Using a medical-grade microneedling pen set to 0.25mm depth, we apply the diluted neurotoxin as the device creates micro-channels across the treatment area. The channels drive the solution into the dermis while triggering your skin's natural healing response.

3

Collagen Remodeling Begins

The micro-injury triggers collagen and elastin production over the next 2 weeks. Meanwhile, the neurotoxin begins regulating sebum and tightening pores within 5-7 days. Peak results appear at 2 weeks -- right when your skin is glowing hardest.

Standard microneedling uses a serum (often hyaluronic acid, growth factors, or vitamin C) to take advantage of the micro-channels created by the needles. MesoTox uses hyperdiluted neurotoxin instead. This adds a completely different layer of benefit: sebum regulation and pore tightening that regular serums can't provide. You still get all the collagen-stimulating benefits of microneedling, plus the dermal neurotoxin effects on top -- it's an upgrade in a single step.

MesoTox is not appropriate if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a known allergy to botulinum toxin products, have active skin infections or breakouts in the treatment area, or are currently taking certain blood thinners. It's also not recommended if you have a neuromuscular condition such as myasthenia gravis. During your consultation, Brittany will review your health history to confirm you're a good candidate and customize the treatment plan for you.
