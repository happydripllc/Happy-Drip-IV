What It Does

Potential Benefits of Red Light Therapy

Individual results vary. Your provider will help determine if red light therapy is appropriate for your goals.

✨

Skin Tone & Texture

May support collagen production and improve the appearance of fine lines, uneven skin tone, and dullness over a series of sessions.

Inflammation Support

Near-infrared wavelengths penetrate deeper tissue and may help reduce localized inflammation and support faster post-workout recovery.

Cellular Energy

Red light is absorbed by mitochondria, which may support ATP production -- the fuel your cells run on -- helping you feel more energized.

💪

Muscle Recovery

Athletes and active patients use red light to support faster muscle recovery and reduce soreness after intense training sessions.

Relaxation & Mood

Many patients find sessions calming. Red light therapy is a quiet, restful experience with no discomfort or side effects for most people.

🚫

No Downtime

Sessions take 10-20 minutes. No recovery period, no UV exposure, no peeling or redness. Return to your normal day immediately after.
