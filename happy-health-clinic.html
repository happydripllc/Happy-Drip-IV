So What Is DPC Anyway?

Healthcare That Actually Feels Good.

Direct Primary Care (DPC) is a simple, refreshing model: you pay one flat monthly membership fee and get direct access to your primary care provider — plus real membership perks like at-cost labs, discounted IV & wellness treatments, and at-cost POC testing.

As part of the Happy Drip IV & Wellness family, Happy Health Clinic is your first stop on the road to wellness. Think of Brittany as your personal health cheerleader — handling everything from preventative screenings to chronic conditions, all with a warm, non-judgmental approach.

  • Preventive care — physicals, screenings, Pap tests, referrals
  • Acute illnesses — colds, sore throats, rashes, UTIs
  • Chronic conditions — hypertension, diabetes, thyroid, PCOS, asthma
  • Mental health support — anxiety and depression management
  • Care coordination and specialist referrals
  • Telehealth and in-person visits available

How It Works

Joining Happy Health Clinic is simple. Three steps to better, more personal healthcare.

📞 Call or Text

2

🩺 Consult

During your consultation, we'll build a plan together that fits your short- and long-term health goals, including exploring treatment options that are right for you.

3

✅ Follow-Up & Thrive
