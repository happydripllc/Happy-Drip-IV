Understanding Your Options

PRP vs. PRF -- What's the Difference?

Both start with a small draw of your own blood and a spin in a centrifuge -- but they produce different concentrations of growth factors with different release patterns.

Standard

PRP -- Platelet-Rich Plasma

A liquid concentrate of platelets and growth factors separated from your blood. PRP delivers a fast, potent release of growth factors at the injection site. It's highly effective for skin rejuvenation, acne scarring, and certain hair restoration protocols. PRP has decades of clinical research behind it and is one of the most well-studied regenerative treatments available.

Advanced

PRF -- Platelet-Rich Fibrin

A newer, more advanced formulation that creates a fibrin matrix -- essentially a slow-release scaffold that delivers growth factors over days rather than hours. PRF contains a higher concentration of platelets and also includes white blood cells and stem cells, which may support longer-lasting tissue regeneration. Many providers consider PRF the evolution of PRP.

What Can PRP / PRF Do for You?

Growth factors are versatile. Here are some of the most popular ways we use PRP and PRF at our Bedford, TX clinic:

✨

Skin Rejuvenation

Injected or microneedled into the skin, PRP/PRF stimulates collagen production and cellular turnover -- improving skin texture, tone, and radiance. Often called the "Vampire Facial" when combined with microneedling.

🌀

Acne Scarring

Growth factors help remodel scar tissue and rebuild collagen in depressed or pitted acne scars, smoothing skin texture over a series of treatments.

💧

Fine Lines & Wrinkles

PRP/PRF doesn't freeze muscles like neurotoxins -- instead it rebuilds the skin from within, softening superficial lines by restoring volume and elasticity.

🌱

Hair Restoration

Injected directly into the scalp, PRP/PRF growth factors stimulate dormant follicles, slow further loss, and support new growth. Often combined with other hair restoration protocols for best results.

🌙

Under-Eye Rejuvenation

The delicate under-eye area responds beautifully to PRF -- thinning, crepey under-eye skin can appear firmer, brighter, and more youthful over a series of treatments.

🔗

Combined Treatments

PRP/PRF amplifies results when paired with microneedling, laser, or other treatments. Ask us about combination protocols to maximize your outcomes.

Simple Process, Powerful Results

1

Consult & Plan

We review your skin goals, medical history, and current treatments to determine whether PRP, PRF, or a combined protocol is the right fit for you.

2

Blood Draw

A small sample of your blood (similar to a routine lab draw) is collected right here in our Bedford clinic -- quick and comfortable.

3

Process & Concentrate

Your blood is spun in a centrifuge to isolate and concentrate the platelet-rich plasma or fibrin -- the good stuff your skin and scalp need.

Apply & Glow

PRP/PRF is applied via injection or microneedling directly to the target area. Minimal downtime -- most clients return to their day within hours.
