The Science

How Laser Tattoo Removal Works

Laser tattoo removal works by delivering high-intensity pulses of light energy into the skin. The laser targets the pigment in tattoo ink, shattering it into tiny fragments small enough for your immune system to naturally flush away over time.

Different ink colors absorb different wavelengths of light -- which is why darker inks (black, navy, dark green) tend to respond faster than lighter or more complex colors. Each session fades the tattoo progressively, with full removal taking multiple treatments depending on the size, age, colors, and depth of the ink.

Your Journey

What to Expect Each Step

1

Free Consult

We assess the tattoo's size, colors, ink depth, age, and location to estimate the number of sessions and set realistic expectations.

2

Laser Session

We apply a cooling device before and during the session. The laser delivers rapid pulses to shatter ink particles. Most sessions take just minutes.

3

Heal & Wait

Your immune system gets to work flushing fragmented ink. You'll notice fading between sessions. Wait 6--8 weeks before your next treatment.

4

Clear Skin

Repeat until you reach your goal -- whether that's complete removal or a lighter base ready for a fresh cover-up design.
