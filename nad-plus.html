What Is NAD+

The coenzyme behind everything your cells do.

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every living cell. It's essential for converting food into energy, repairing damaged DNA, regulating your circadian rhythm, and maintaining cellular health. Without enough NAD+, your cells can't function efficiently.

The problem: NAD+ levels naturally decline with age. By your 40s, you may have half the NAD+ you had in your 20s. This decline is linked to fatigue, brain fog, poor recovery, accelerated aging, and increased vulnerability to metabolic dysfunction.

NAD+ therapy replenishes what time takes away -- delivered directly into your system via IV infusion for immediate impact or subcutaneous injection for sustained support. This is one of the most requested longevity treatments Brittany offers, and one she uses herself.

What NAD+ Does For You

The benefits of restoring NAD+

Energy Production

NAD+ is required for mitochondria to convert food into ATP -- the energy currency of every cell. More NAD+ means more efficient energy output.

DNA Repair

NAD+ activates sirtuins and PARP enzymes that repair damaged DNA. This is one of the core mechanisms behind cellular aging and longevity research.

🧠

Cognitive Clarity

Brain fog, poor focus, and mental fatigue are common signs of NAD+ depletion. Restoring levels supports neurotransmitter function and mental sharpness.

🔄

Recovery and Repair

Faster recovery from workouts, illness, and procedures. NAD+ supports the cellular repair pathways that handle tissue turnover and healing.

⏳

Anti-Aging at the Cellular Level

NAD+ activates sirtuins -- the "longevity genes" that regulate inflammation, metabolism, and stress resistance. This is aging intervention at the source.

😴

Sleep and Circadian Rhythm

NAD+ plays a direct role in regulating your internal clock. Patients commonly report improved sleep quality and more consistent energy throughout the day.
