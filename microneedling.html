The Science

Collagen Induction -- Your Skin's Repair Mode

Microneedling (also called collagen induction therapy) uses a device with ultra-fine needles to create controlled micro-injuries in the skin. Your body responds by flooding the area with growth factors and new collagen -- the same proteins that keep skin firm, smooth, and youthful.

The result is tighter, more even-toned skin with visibly reduced pores, fine lines, and scarring -- all using your own biology. Add one of our three regenerative boosters and you accelerate results significantly.

  • Reduces acne scars, surgical scars, and stretch marks
  • Minimizes enlarged pores and uneven texture
  • Smooths fine lines and mild wrinkles
  • Improves hyperpigmentation and sun damage
  • Enhances absorption of topical serums

Your Options

Three Ways to Boost Your Results

Standard microneedling is powerful on its own. Pair it with one of our regenerative add-ons to supercharge healing and take results to the next level.

Vampire Facial

PRP Microneedling

Platelet-Rich Plasma

We draw a small amount of your blood, spin it in a centrifuge to concentrate the growth-factor-rich plasma, and apply it directly during your microneedling session. The micro-channels deliver PRP deep into the dermis where it works best.

  • Uses your own biology -- zero foreign substances
  • Concentrated growth factors accelerate tissue repair
  • Ideal for acne scars, dullness, and early aging
  • Pairs especially well with hair restoration too

Results build over 4--6 weeks as collagen remodeling continues. Most patients see best results after a series of 3 sessions.

Exosomes

Exosome Microneedling

Next-Gen Cell Signaling

Exosomes are nano-sized extracellular vesicles that carry regenerative signals between cells. Applied to skin after microneedling, they instruct surrounding cells to ramp up collagen production, reduce inflammation, and accelerate repair -- faster and more consistently than PRP for many patients.

  • Billions of growth factors and signaling proteins per vial
  • No blood draw required -- ready immediately
  • Excellent for sensitive skin or those who want a faster session
  • Potent anti-inflammatory properties reduce downtime

Many patients choose exosomes for their consistency and the reduced redness compared to PRP. Ideal for first-timers.

Cellvane

Cellvane Microneedling

Advanced Regenerative Serum

Cellvane is a bioactive serum formulated with growth factors, peptides, and skin-regenerating actives designed specifically to be delivered via microneedling. It works synergistically with the micro-channel delivery system to optimize skin renewal at the cellular level.

  • Precision-formulated for transdermal delivery
  • Targets fine lines, laxity, and uneven texture
  • No blood draw -- convenient same-day treatment
  • Strong option for mature or sun-damaged skin

Ask your provider which booster is best matched to your specific skin goals at your consultation.

What to Expect

Your Treatment, Step by Step

1

Consultation

We review your skin concerns, goals, and history to choose the right microneedling depth and add-on for your specific needs.

2

Numbing

A topical numbing cream is applied and left on for 30--45 minutes so you're comfortable throughout the session.

3

Treatment

The microneedling device passes over your skin while your chosen booster (PRP, Exosomes, or Cellvane) is applied simultaneously.

4

Aftercare

You'll leave with post-care instructions. Expect mild redness for 24--72 hours. Results improve over 4--6 weeks as collagen builds.

Is This Right for Me?

Good Candidates & Considerations

Microneedling works for a wide range of skin types and concerns. Here's a quick guide -- but your provider will give you a personalized recommendation at your consultation.

Great Candidates

  • ✓Acne scars or surgical scars
  • ✓Fine lines and early skin laxity
  • ✓Enlarged pores or rough texture
  • ✓Hyperpigmentation or sun damage
  • ✓Dull or uneven skin tone
  • ✓All Fitzpatrick skin types (I--VI)

When to Wait or Discuss First

  • !Active acne breakouts in the treatment area
  • !Current use of isotretinoin (Accutane) or recent history
  • !Keloid scar tendency
  • !Pregnancy or nursing
  • !Active skin infection or open wounds
  • !Certain blood-thinning medications (discuss at consult)
