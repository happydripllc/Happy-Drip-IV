The Problem

Your Employees Shouldn't Lose Half a Day Just to See a Doctor.

Your employees are the heartbeat of your business, and their health is the foundation of your success.

Getting routine primary care is harder than it should be. Finding an office that can see someone same-day means calling around. Urgent care is available -- but it means a wait and a provider who doesn't know the patient. Follow-up care gets scattered across different offices and different faces.

For small businesses, that friction has a real cost. When one person is out, it's usually not invisible. Happy Health Clinic gives your employees a simpler place to start.

Sick today?

Managing blood pressure, thyroid, or a chronic condition?

Keep care connected, not scattered across providers who each only see part of the picture.

Need telehealth?

Use it when it's clinically the right fit -- with the same provider who already knows you.

What Is DPC?

So... What Exactly Is Direct Primary Care?

Direct Primary Care (DPC) removes the barriers between your employees and the care they need.

Instead of billing insurance for routine primary care, members pay a predictable monthly fee directly to Happy Health Clinic. That membership gives employees access to the primary-care services included in the program -- no per-visit copays for included care.

It removes a lot of friction between your employee and their provider. That's most of the idea.

Small businesses can offer DPC as a standalone employee benefit or alongside a traditional health plan. Either way, it gives employees a primary-care practice that's set up to actually know them.

Important to understand

Direct Primary Care Is Not Health Insurance.

A DPC membership provides access to primary care services within the scope of the program. It does not replace coverage for emergencies, hospitalizations, specialist visits, major procedures, surgery, or services outside the membership. Employees still need separate coverage for those.

DPC can work alongside an existing group health plan to give employees easier access to routine primary care. For small businesses that don't currently offer a group plan, it can serve as a meaningful primary-care benefit while employees maintain their own separate coverage for broader needs.

We don't provide tax, ERISA, ACA, or insurance advice. Talk to your benefits advisor or accountant about how DPC fits your specific situation.

Flexible Participation

Choose How Your Business Contributes.

The membership price stays the same. How your business participates is up to you.

01

Employer-Paid

The company covers the full monthly membership for each enrolled employee. Employees receive the benefit at no cost to them.

Good for employers who want to make the benefit genuinely accessible -- a standout benefit that employees can actually use.

02

Shared Contribution

The employer contributes a fixed amount toward each membership. The employee pays the remaining balance directly.

Example: employer contributes $50/month. Employee pays the remaining $50 for ages 21--54.
Happy Health Clinic receives the full applicable membership amount regardless of how it's split.
03

Voluntary Benefit

The business makes Happy Health Clinic membership available to eligible employees. Employees elect whether to enroll and pay for the benefit themselves.

A good option for employers who want to offer access without taking on the monthly cost.

The right structure for your business depends on your specific situation. We recommend discussing benefit structure, potential tax treatment, and any applicable ERISA considerations with your accountant or benefits advisor before setting up a program. We're happy to answer questions about how the membership works -- but we don't provide tax, legal, or insurance advice.
