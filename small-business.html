What Is DPC?

Simple Access. Real Results.

Direct Primary Care (DPC) removes the barriers between your employees and the care they need. One flat monthly fee gives your team direct access to primary care — without the insurance runaround, long wait times, or frustrating referral loops.

📈

Boosted Productivity

Minor health issues resolved quickly — same-day access means employees aren't waiting weeks to be seen. Fewer missed shifts. Faster returns. Less disruption to your team.

💰

Significant Savings

Early intervention avoids expensive ER visits and unnecessary urgent care trips. When employees have a place to turn before things escalate, everyone saves — you and them.

😌

Reduced Stress

Care without traditional insurance frustration or long wait times. Your team gets real answers from a real provider — without spending half the day in a waiting room.

🌟

Attract & Retain Talent

High-quality, personal healthcare is a powerful differentiator. Offering a DPC benefit sets you apart as an employer who genuinely invests in their people.
