Botox vs. Jeuveau: What's the Difference?

By Brittany Adams, APRN FNP-C · Happy Drip IV & Wellness, Bedford, TX

If you've been researching neurotoxin treatments, you've probably seen both Botox and Jeuveau come up. They do the same thing -- relax the muscles that cause wrinkles -- but they're made by different companies, have slightly different formulations, and come at different price points.

We use Jeuveau at Happy Drip, and I get this question constantly. Here's the full breakdown.

How Neurotoxins Work

Both Botox and Jeuveau are botulinum toxin type A injections. They work by temporarily blocking the nerve signals that tell certain facial muscles to contract. No contractions means the skin above those muscles stays smooth instead of creasing into lines.

This is why they're effective for expression lines -- forehead lines, frown lines (the "11s" between the brows), and crow's feet. These wrinkles form because the same muscles have been firing the same way for years. Relaxing those muscles gives the skin a chance to smooth out.

What Makes Them Different

Botox is made by Allergan (AbbVie) and has been FDA-approved since 2002. It's the original and most recognized name in the space -- but that brand recognition comes with a higher price tag.

Jeuveau is made by Evolus and received FDA approval in 2019. It was the first neurotoxin approved specifically for cosmetic use only -- meaning the company put all of its research, manufacturing, and clinical work into aesthetic applications rather than splitting focus with medical uses. The result is a modern, highly purified formulation that performs comparably to Botox in clinical trials.

Does Jeuveau Work as Well as Botox?

Yes. The clinical trial that supported Jeuveau's FDA approval showed it performed equivalent to Botox for glabellar lines (the 11s) in head-to-head comparison. Both produced meaningful wrinkle reduction; both lasted about the same duration.

In practice, patients who come to me after years of Botox almost universally report similar or equivalent results with Jeuveau. The main difference they notice? The price.

Why We Use Jeuveau

I chose Jeuveau for Happy Drip for a few reasons:

  • Cost to patients. At $9/unit, Jeuveau gives patients access to quality neurotoxin treatment without the premium brand markup. A typical forehead + 11s treatment runs 30-50 units -- that's a meaningful difference per session.
  • It's purpose-built for aesthetics. Every clinical investment Evolus made went into cosmetic outcomes. That focus shows in the product.
  • Comparable clinical results. I wouldn't use anything that didn't perform. The data and my patient outcomes both back it up.

How Many Units Do You Need?

It depends on the area and your anatomy. General ranges:

  • Forehead lines: 10-20 units
  • Glabellar lines (11s): 15-25 units
  • Crow's feet: 10-15 units per side
  • Lip flip: 4-6 units
  • Brow lift: 4-8 units

I assess your muscle strength and movement during your appointment and recommend an amount based on your specific anatomy and the result you're going for -- not a one-size number.

What to Expect at Your Appointment

The appointment itself is quick -- typically 15-20 minutes. I'll go over your goals, assess your movement, mark the injection sites, and administer the injections. There's minimal discomfort (most people describe it as a quick pinch) and no downtime. You can go back to your normal day immediately.

Results start to appear in 3-5 days and are typically fully developed within 2 weeks. Plan your appointment accordingly if you have an event.
