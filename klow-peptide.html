What Is KLOW

One blend. Four pathways. Coordinated healing.

KLOW is a multi-peptide blend formulated to address healing, inflammation, tissue repair, and cellular renewal simultaneously. Instead of targeting one pathway at a time, KLOW combines four clinically studied peptides into a single subcutaneous injection protocol.

Each component works on a different mechanism -- gut healing, tissue repair, anti-inflammation, collagen remodeling -- but they converge toward the same goal: getting your body's repair systems back online and running at full capacity.

Brittany prescribes KLOW through a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy with individualized dosing based on your labs, goals, and health history. This is a real prescription, not a grey-market product.

  • One injection per day instead of managing multiple vials, doses, and schedules separately.

The Four Components

What's inside KLOW -- and what each one does

Each peptide in the KLOW blend serves a distinct role. Together, they create a coordinated regenerative signal that's greater than the sum of its parts.

🩹

BPC-157 Body Protection Compound

A pentadecapeptide originally derived from gastric proteins. BPC-157 supports gut lining integrity, connective tissue repair, and angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation). It's one of the most clinically studied healing peptides and serves as the foundation of the KLOW blend.

Gut Healing Tissue Repair Angiogenesis
🔥

KPV Anti-Inflammatory Tripeptide

A tripeptide fragment of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (alpha-MSH). KPV works directly inside the cell nucleus to inhibit NF-kB and MAPK -- two master switches that control chronic inflammation. This is the "calm down" signal in the blend.

NF-kB Inhibition Inflammation Control Immune Modulation
🔄

TB-500 Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment

A synthetic analog of thymosin beta-4, a protein involved in cell migration, new vessel formation, and organized wound repair. TB-500 has systemic distribution -- it doesn't just work locally. It travels to wherever the body needs repair support.

Cell Migration Wound Repair Systemic Distribution
✨

GHK-Cu Copper Peptide

A naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide found in human plasma. GHK-Cu drives collagen production, remodels the extracellular matrix, and breaks down damaged collagen so healthy tissue can replace it. The anti-aging and skin quality component of the blend.

Collagen Production Matrix Remodeling Cellular Renewal

KLOW Peptide FAQs

KLOW is the brand name for a specific 4-peptide blend containing BPC-157, KPV, TB-500, and GHK-Cu. The name refers to the blend itself rather than being an acronym. It's formulated so that all four components can be delivered in a single daily subcutaneous injection rather than managing multiple separate peptide protocols.

Single-peptide protocols target one pathway. KLOW targets four simultaneously: inflammation control (KPV), barrier repair (BPC-157), systemic tissue repair (TB-500), and collagen remodeling (GHK-Cu). The components are synergistic -- for example, KPV calms the inflammation that's damaging tissue while BPC-157 rebuilds the damaged tissue. You're addressing the full repair cycle rather than one piece of it. It also simplifies dosing: one injection per day instead of managing multiple vials and schedules.

Most patients begin noticing changes within the first 2-3 weeks -- particularly with inflammation reduction, sleep quality, and general energy. Tissue repair and gut healing benefits typically become more apparent in weeks 4-6. Collagen remodeling and skin quality improvements from GHK-Cu are more gradual and compound over the full 8-week protocol and beyond. Every patient's timeline varies based on what we're targeting and your starting point.
