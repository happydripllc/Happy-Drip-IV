What We Treat

Erectile Dysfunction Is More Common Than You Think

Erectile dysfunction affects men of all ages -- and it's a medical issue, not a personal failure. Whether it's occasional difficulty or a consistent pattern, it's worth addressing with a qualified provider who will actually listen.

At Happy Drip IV & Wellness, Brittany Adams, APRN FNP-C provides a thorough evaluation and, when appropriate, prescribes FDA-approved medications to help you get back to feeling like yourself. Everything is handled in a private, judgment-free visit.

ED can also be an early warning sign of cardiovascular issues, so a proper medical evaluation isn't just helpful -- it's important.

Symptoms We Address

  • Difficulty achieving or maintaining an erection
  • Reduced sexual confidence or performance anxiety
  • Decreased libido alongside ED
  • ED related to stress, fatigue, or lifestyle factors
  • ED as a symptom of low testosterone (we treat both)
  • Inconsistent response to previously prescribed medications
Your Options

Sildenafil vs. Tadalafil -- Which Is Right for You?

Both medications work by increasing blood flow to the penis. The key differences are onset time and how long the effect lasts. Your provider will recommend the best fit based on your lifestyle and preferences.

Sildenafil

Generic for Viagra®
Onset 30--60 minutes
Duration 4--6 hours
Dosing As-needed (25--100mg)
Food Best on empty stomach
Best for Planned intimacy

Works well when you have a predictable window. Take it 30--60 min before activity and avoid high-fat meals, which can slow absorption.

Tadalafil

Generic for Cialis®
Onset 30 minutes--2 hours
Duration Up to 36 hours
Dosing As-needed or daily (2.5--20mg)
Food Not affected by food
Best for Spontaneous intimacy or daily use

The longer window removes the pressure to "plan." Can also be taken daily at a low dose to maintain readiness around the clock.
