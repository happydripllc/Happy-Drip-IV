Why This Gets Missed

Two Conditions That Get Dismissed More Than Almost Anything Else

PCOS and thyroid dysfunction often travel together, share overlapping symptoms, and are two of the most commonly under-tested, under-treated conditions in women's health. A single TSH that comes back "in range" or a period that's merely irregular (not absent) is often where the workup stops — even when weight, energy, skin, hair, and mood all say otherwise.

We start with a full panel — not just the one or two markers that get ordered by default — and walk you through exactly what your results mean before building a plan around them.

Is This You?

Signs Worth Getting Checked

These symptoms overlap more than you'd think — which is exactly why a full panel matters more than guessing which condition you have.

Signs of PCOS

  • Irregular, infrequent, or absent periods
  • Difficulty losing weight despite consistent effort
  • Acne along the jawline, chin, or chest
  • Excess facial or body hair growth
  • Thinning hair on the scalp
  • Difficulty getting pregnant
  • Skin tags or darkened skin patches (neck, armpits)

Signs of Thyroid Dysfunction

  • Persistent fatigue, even with enough sleep
  • Unexplained weight gain or difficulty losing weight
  • Feeling cold when others don't
  • Hair thinning or hair loss
  • Dry skin, brittle nails
  • Brain fog or difficulty concentrating
  • Mood changes, anxiety, or depression
