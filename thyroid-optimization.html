Is This You?

Signs Worth Getting Checked

Thyroid symptoms fall into two very different patterns depending on whether your thyroid is underactive or overactive — here's what to look for.

Underactive Thyroid

Hypothyroidism
  • Persistent fatigue, even with enough sleep
  • Unexplained weight gain or trouble losing weight
  • Feeling cold when others aren't
  • Dry skin, brittle nails, hair thinning or loss
  • Brain fog, low mood, or depression
  • Constipation

Overactive Thyroid

Hyperthyroidism
  • Unexplained weight loss despite normal eating
  • Racing heart, palpitations, or anxiety
  • Feeling hot, excess sweating
  • Hand tremors or restlessness
  • Trouble sleeping
  • Frequent bowel movements

Important Considerations

  • ✗Thyroid disease is managed long-term, not with a single visit
  • ✗Suspected thyroid nodules or structural concerns require imaging and, if needed, a specialist referral
  • ✗Severe hyperthyroidism (like thyroid storm) is an emergency — not something managed here
  • ✗Regular follow-up labs are required to safely adjust medication doses
